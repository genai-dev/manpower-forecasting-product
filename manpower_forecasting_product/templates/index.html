Manpower & Business Forecasting Platform

Certified historical data → controlled training → forecast → what-if scenarios → LLM insights.

1. One-time bootstrap

Loads FY24-FY26 legacy Manpower/Exit and Business accounting files, canonicalizes them and saves versioned certified datasets.

POST /api/bootstrap

2. Future monthly upload

Business uploads only new actual month(s) using the standard workbook. It does not need to upload the historical three years again.

POST /api/upload/monthly

3. Controlled training

Retraining is separate from upload and uses only validated actual data.

POST /api/train

4. Forecast

Closing, Exit, derived Opening/Hiring/Attrition, NOP and ATS.

POST /api/forecast

5. Scenario + insights

Temporary overrides never enter training data. Compare baseline vs scenario and send computed facts to the LLM for explanation.
